Detailed Itinerary

The 15-Day Journey Roadmap

Day 1

Arrival in Bali & Sunset at Uluwatu Temple

Arrive at Denpasar Airport, transfer to hotel and enjoy a stunning sunset at Uluwatu Temple with traditional Kecak dance performance.

Hotel Transfer Uluwatu Temple Dinner Included
Day 2

Ubud Cultural Heart & Rice Terraces

Explore the lush Tegallalang Rice Terraces, Ubud Monkey Forest sanctuary, sacred Tirta Empul water purification fountains, and an authentic Balinese culinary workshop.

Rice Terraces Hike Sacred Water Cleansing Breakfast & Lunch
Day 3

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek & Hot Springs

Early morning guided trek to Mount Batur summit to witness an awe-inspiring sunrise above the cloud bank, followed by soaking in natural volcanic hot springs beside Lake Batur.

Sunrise Summit Trek Lake Batur Hot Springs Summit Breakfast
Day 4

Nusa Penida Island Exploration

Speedboat voyage across Badung Strait to Nusa Penida. Visit the iconic T-Rex cliff at Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong and snorkel with giant manta rays.

Speedboat Tickets Manta Snorkeling Seafood Beach Lunch
Day 5 - 15

Remaining Journey (Days 5 - 15)

Days 5–8: Gili Islands yacht cruise and sea turtle conservation; Days 9–11: North Bali waterfalls, Lovina dolphin tour, and Banjar hot springs; Days 12–15: Seminyak luxury beach retreat, spa treatments, Tanah Lot sunset, and VIP airport departure transfer.

Gili Catamaran Cruise Luxury Spa Days All Breakfasts & Gala Dinner
